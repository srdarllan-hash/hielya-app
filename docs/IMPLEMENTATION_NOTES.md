# C-005 Home implementation notes

- The Home is the Master Screen and validates reusable commerce/navigation patterns.
- ETA is presented as an estimate, never a guarantee.
- Alcohol messaging states that delivery must finish before 22:00; it does not imply checkout remains open until 22:00.
- Products and pack imagery are approved-reference assets extracted for prototype use; production rights and final product photography remain governed by the catalog asset process.
- No component was created exclusively for C-005.
- The bottom navigation remains visible while content scrolls.
- Every interactive element has a minimum 44×44 CSS-pixel target or equivalent padded hit area.
